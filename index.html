Wrap God Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026 · applies to every published version of the extension

Wrap God is a browser extension that adds tools to the work sites used by Freedom Mobile call-centre agents. It has no server of its own. It does not send what it reads to its author or to any analytics service, and everything it remembers is kept in your own browser on your own computer.

This page explains that in full: what the extension can see, what it writes down, how long it keeps it, and every place information can travel to.

The short version

  • There is no Wrap God server. Nothing is uploaded to the developer — not settings, not page content, not usage.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Nothing syncs. The extension uses local storage only, so its data never rides your browser profile to another machine.
  • No remote code is downloaded or run. Everything the extension executes ships inside the package Google reviewed.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it kept.

1. Who this policy is from

Wrap God is built and maintained by its author, David Angel, and published on the Chrome Web Store under that developer account. It is a productivity tool for call-centre agents.

This policy covers the extension itself. The work systems it runs on — the customer management, telephony, workforce and knowledge-base sites listed below — are operated by Freedom Mobile, Quebecor and their suppliers, and are governed by those organisations' own policies, not by this one.

2. Where the extension runs

Wrap God is inert everywhere except a fixed list of work sites. It cannot see, and is never loaded into, any other page you visit — your personal browsing, banking, mail and everything else are outside its reach entirely, by design and by the permissions Google enforces.

The sites it runs on are the customer management system, the agent desktop and telephony client, the workforce-management portal, the internal knowledge base, the internal ordering and activation portals, the public store locator and coverage map, a shipping carrier's tracking page, and an internal Microsoft Forms request form. Two further sites — the self-serve site and the payment portal — are optional: the extension asks for them separately, by name, the first time you use the feature that needs one, and works without them if you say no.

3. What the extension can see

On those sites, Wrap God reads the page you are already looking at. On the customer-facing ones that includes customer account information — names, phone numbers, addresses, account and case details, and notes an agent writes — because that is what the tools work with: filling a note, pre-filling a form, checking a device or an address. On the workforce site it reads your own schedule and handled-call statistics to run the on-screen clock.

It sees this information only while you are signed in and looking at it, and only to do the job the on-screen tool is doing.

4. What is stored, and for how long

Everything below is written to your browser's extension storage on this computer. None of it is transmitted anywhere by the extension.

Your settings Which tools are switched on, theme, clock and reminder preferences, note templates Until you change them, reset, or remove the extension
Call context The note last written into a field, an unsent note draft, a callback entry (case number and callback number), an address being carried between two tabs, a question typed into the knowledge-base search Short-lived by design. Individual items expire on their own — typically between one minute and twelve hours — and are cleared by Reset or by removing the extension
Cached reference data Rate plans, device compatibility lists, store locations, international rates, tax tables Refreshed periodically. Contains no personal information
Diagnostics A rolling on-screen log of the last few dozen call-handling events on this machine, used to troubleshoot one specific telephony feature Rolling window, this machine only. Never uploaded; shared only if you choose to copy it and send it yourself

The extension keeps no history of the calls you have taken, no record of which customers you have looked at, and no log of what you do with it.

6. What the extension never does

  • It never collects or transmits personal information to its developer.
  • It never sells or shares information with third parties, and it is never used for advertising, credit-scoring, lending or any purpose unrelated to the tool's single function.
  • It never records audio or video. It can measure the loudness of a call already playing through your browser tab, to tell hold music from a voice; the sound is analysed as it plays and nothing is ever captured, saved or sent.
  • It never reads your passwords or credentials, and never asks for any.
  • It never touches pages outside its site list.

7. Settings backup and restore

The extension can export your settings to a file so you can move them to another machine, and import one back. The export is deliberately built to carry preferences only: anything that came from a call is excluded, including note drafts, callback entries with case and phone numbers, addresses in transit, typed knowledge-base questions, and the case number and callback number attached to a reminder. The file is saved by your browser to your own downloads — it is never uploaded anywhere.

9. Your choices

  • Every tool can be switched off individually in the extension's settings.
  • Reset in settings clears what the extension has stored.
  • The optional site permissions can be withdrawn at any time from your browser's extension settings page.
  • Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data from your computer. Because nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing left elsewhere to delete.

10. Work accounts and employer systems

Wrap God is used on employer-provided systems with employer-provided accounts. What those systems themselves record about your session, and what your employer may do with it, is a matter between you and your employer — this policy covers only the extension's own behaviour. Use of the extension does not change your obligations under your employer's policies for handling customer information.

11. Children

Wrap God is a workplace tool for call-centre agents. It is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. The published address stays the same, so the version you are reading is always the current one.
